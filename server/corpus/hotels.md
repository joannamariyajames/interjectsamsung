# Interject Travel (fictional demo agency) - Hotels & Stays

## Rate types
Flexible rates cancel free until 18:00 local time on the day of arrival.
Advance Purchase rates are 22 percent cheaper and non-refundable. Corporate
rates require a company code and include breakfast for two. These are the rate
types Interject Travel offers at its partner hotels.

## Properties
The Harbour House in Mumbai sits 20 minutes from the domestic terminal, rates
from 8,900 INR, rooftop pool, 24-hour gym, free airport shuttle at :15 past the
hour. The Cantonment in Delhi is 45 minutes from Terminal 3, rates from 7,400
INR, no pool, strong business centre. Lantern Court in Bengaluru is 35 minutes
from the airport, rates from 6,200 INR, quiet courtyard rooms recommended for
light sleepers. All three are fictional Interject Travel partner properties
made up for this demo; they are not real hotels.

## Check-in rules
Standard check-in is 14:00 and check-out is 11:00. Early check-in from 09:00
costs half a night. Late check-out to 16:00 is free on Corporate rates. These
rules apply at Interject Travel partner hotels.
