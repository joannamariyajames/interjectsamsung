# Interject Travel (fictional demo agency) - Client Travel Policy & Expenses

## Approval thresholds
For corporate clients enrolled with Interject Travel, domestic trips under
40,000 INR total are auto-approved. Anything above needs a manager sign-off
recorded before ticketing. International travel always needs sign-off
regardless of amount. This applies only to travellers whose employer has an
Interject Travel corporate account, never to anyone else by default.

## Per diem
Under an Interject Travel corporate account, metro cities are 2,600 INR per day
and other cities 1,800 INR per day. Per diem is halved on days where a
conference provides meals.

## Reimbursement
Expense claims for trips booked through an Interject Travel corporate account
must be filed within 30 days of trip completion. Receipts are required above
1,000 INR. Ride-hailing receipts must show the pickup and drop addresses.
Alcohol is never reimbursable.

## Refunds
Refunds on cancelled Interject Travel bookings land back on the original
payment method in 5 to 7 business days. Refunds to a corporate card go to the
company account, not to the traveller.
