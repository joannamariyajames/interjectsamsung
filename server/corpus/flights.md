# Interject Travel (fictional demo agency) - Flights & Fare Rules

## Cabin classes
Economy Saver is the cheapest bucket. It is non-refundable but changeable once
for a fee of 2,400 INR plus fare difference. Economy Flex is fully refundable up
to 4 hours before departure. Business includes two free changes and lounge
access at all hub airports. These are Interject Travel's own fare buckets for
bookings made through the agency, not any airline's published rules.

## Change and cancellation windows
Changes made more than 72 hours before departure incur no service fee on Flex
fares. Inside 72 hours a 1,200 INR handling charge applies. Cancellations inside
2 hours of departure forfeit the fare on every bucket except Business. These
windows apply to tickets issued by Interject Travel.

## Baggage
Economy Saver includes 15 kg checked and 7 kg cabin. Economy Flex includes 25 kg
checked. Business includes 40 kg checked across two pieces. Excess baggage is
charged at 550 INR per kilogram at the airport and 400 INR per kilogram if
pre-purchased online. Allowances are Interject Travel's fare-bucket defaults;
the operating airline's own rules take precedence on the day.

## Schedules and live fares
Interject Travel keeps no flight schedules, departure times, frequencies or
live prices: those change daily and are checked in a live airline search at
the moment of booking. The routes its clients book most often are Bengaluru to
Delhi, Bengaluru to Mumbai and Delhi to Mumbai. For times, flight counts and
today's fares on any route, check the airline or a live booking site.
